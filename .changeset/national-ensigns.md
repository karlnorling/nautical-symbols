Add national ensigns and jacks as a new `ensigns` group, with three categories:

- `civil-ensigns`: civil, government, reserve and yacht ensigns, per country.
- `naval-ensigns`: naval, naval auxiliary and coast guard ensigns, per country.
- `jacks`: naval and civil jacks, per country.

They come from the galleries in the Wikipedia articles "Civil ensign", "Naval ensign" and "Jack (flag)". Former and historical flags are left out. `NauticalSymbolGroup` now includes `'ensigns'`.
